## Project Overview

**From Browsing to Buying: Product Engagement, Visitor Type, and Online Purchase Conversion**

This contribution prepares the analytical dataset and explores browsing behavior and purchase conversion among new and returning online shoppers. The findings support the team's subsequent logistic regression, interaction analysis, and model diagnostics.

**Research question:** How does the relationship between engagement with product-related pages and the likelihood of completing an online purchase differ between new and returning visitors, after accounting for other browsing behaviors and session characteristics?

## Data

Source: [UCI Online Shoppers Purchasing Intention Dataset](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/468/online) ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5F88Q)). Each observation represents a browsing session.

## Data Preparation Decisions

- No imputation was necessary because no missing values were identified.
- Excluded 85 sessions classified as `Other`, restricting the analysis to new and returning visitors.
- Retained identical records provisionally because no unique session identifiers are available. The original data contained 125 duplicate rows beyond their first occurrences.
- Did not remove observations solely because of zero browsing durations or potential IQR outliers.
- Recoded `Revenue` as 0 (no purchase) and 1 (purchase); converted categorical predictors to factors with `New_Visitor` as the visitor-type reference level.
- Added `Duration_Minutes` and `Log_Duration = log1p(Duration_Minutes)` while preserving the original duration variable.

## EDA Results

| Metric | New visitors | Returning visitors |
|---|---:|---:|
| Sessions | 1,694 | 10,551 |
| Purchasing sessions | 422 | 1,470 |
| Conversion rate | 24.91% | 13.93% |
| Mean product browsing duration (minutes) | 10.61 | 21.49 |

- Overall conversion rate: 15.45% (1,892 purchasing sessions).
- Conversion generally increased with product browsing duration in both visitor groups. The longest duration group included only 15 new-visitor sessions, so its conversion rate should be interpreted cautiously.
- In the original sample, log transformation reduced duration skewness from 7.262 to -0.076.
- Bounce rate and exit rate (r = 0.913) and product page count and duration (r = 0.861) showed strong correlations in the analytical sample.

These are unadjusted descriptive findings, not causal conclusions or evidence of a statistically significant interaction.
